PPP / Concession Model

Three PPP projects over a 30-year concession: a hospital PFI, a shadow toll road and a schools project. Model the payment mechanism with deductions, build the public sector comparator, test value for money, and run the cash flow waterfall, debt service and equity returns.

The problem it solves

A PPP has two audiences who read the same model differently. The authority wants to know whether the deal beats public procurement. Investors and lenders want to know whether the unitary charge covers debt service with room for a dividend.

This model answers both from one set of inputs: a public sector comparator with optimism bias and risk adjustments against the PPP cost to the authority, and a project cash flow waterfall with DSCR, a debt service reserve and a dividend lock-up test.

Who it's for

Project finance and infrastructure teams at sponsors, lenders and advisers. Public sector finance teams assessing value for money. Infrastructure investors reviewing a PPP portfolio, and students of project finance.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

PSC discount rate, inflation, tax, base rate, CAPM inputs, deduction rate, demand growth and cost overrun factor; for each project its construction cost, concession, availability payment, opex, lifecycle, debt and equity, demand base, third-party income, DSRA and lock-up DSCR; and the PSC risk and optimism bias percentages. Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

PSC_BENCHMARK

Public sector comparator per project: raw cost, optimism bias, retained, transferred and transferable risk, competitive neutrality, and its NPV.

CONCESSION_STRUCT

The payment mechanism: availability payment by component, performance deductions, shadow toll or demand payment, third-party income and inflation uplift.

REVENUE_FORECAST

60-period revenue per project, in real and nominal terms.

LIFECYCLE_CAPEX

Annual lifecycle budget, five-year major maintenance and a reserve fund with contributions, drawdowns and interest.

FINANCING_STRUCT

Senior and mezzanine debt, equity, gearing, blended cost of debt, CAPM cost of equity, WACC, tenor, DSRA months and the interest rate hedge.

CASH_FLOWS

The waterfall per project: revenue, opex, lifecycle, debt service, tax, DSRA, the lock-up test, dividends and cash balance.

DEBT_SERVICE

Drawdown, interest, repayment, DSCR, approximate LLCR and the DSRA per project.

VFM_ANALYSIS

PSC NPV against PPP NPV, the value for money saving, a breakeven discount rate and sensitivity to the discount rate.

RISK_MATRIX

15 risk types allocated between the public and private sectors, with the mitigant for each and quantified values per project.

RETURNS

Equity multiple, nominal and real equity IRR, NPV at cost of equity, payback, dividend yield, average and minimum DSCR, and LLCR.

DASHBOARD

Value for money, equity IRR, MOIC, minimum DSCR, gearing and dividend yield, with chart data for VfM and the DSCR profile.

SENSITIVITY

VfM by discount rate and deduction rate, equity IRR by availability payment and inflation, and NPV by cost overrun and demand growth.

CHECKS

59 integrity checks, including equity plus debt equal to total sources, debt repaid by maturity and non-negative cash and lifecycle reserves for every project.

What makes it different

Quarterly where it matters

Forty quarterly periods for the first ten years, through construction and early operations, then twenty annual periods to year 30.

Value for money from both sides

PSC with optimism bias, risk adjustments and competitive neutrality against the PPP cost to the authority, with the saving and its sensitivity to the discount rate.

A real payment mechanism

Availability payment less performance deductions, plus demand or shadow toll payments and third-party income, indexed to inflation.

Lender protections modelled

A six-month debt service reserve, a lifecycle reserve fund and a dividend lock-up when DSCR falls below the covenant.

Risk allocation on one page

Fifteen risks, from construction overrun to refinancing, allocated public, private or shared, with the contractual mitigant for each.

Frequently asked questions

Which projects are in the demo?

A hospital PFI, a road on shadow tolls and a schools project, in sterling. Every assumption is an input, so each slot can carry your own project.

What does it not do?

Interest is not calculated on exact dates. Equity IRR uses Excel's IRR on annual cash flows rather than date-based XIRR, LLCR is approximated from DSCR, construction cost is spread evenly, and one inflation rate applies throughout; sector-specific indices are not modelled.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo portfolio, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
