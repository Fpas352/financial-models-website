Five-year monthly bank plan in Excel. Twenty lending products, fourteen deposit products, treasury, wholesale funding, ECL, RWA, capital, LCR and NSFR, all flowing into one P&L, balance sheet and cash flow. One scenario switch.

Who it's for

Bank FP&A leads, Finance Directors, ALM and Treasury teams at community and regional banks. Bank consultancies supporting mid-market clients. Built for the gap between Excel templates and enterprise planning systems.

What's in the model

Control, Time and Toggle

Model settings and definitions, a 60-month timeline on a 30/360 day count, and the scenario switch: Base, Upside or Downside, each moving rates, lending volumes, ECL, deposit growth and operating costs.

Eight input tabs

InpRates, InpLen, InpDep, InpCred, InpCosts, InpTreas, InpReg and InpOther hold every assumption, including Base, Rise and Cut paths for Bank Rate, SONIA and swap rates.

Lending

Twenty products across residential mortgages, buy-to-let, commercial real estate, SME, corporate, consumer, government-guaranteed, development and specialised lending. Each runs a 60-cohort vintage matrix with new business, amortisation, prepayment and write-offs. Fixed-rate books carry a hedge ratio and swap P&L.

Deposits

Fourteen products, from personal and business current accounts through instant access, notice, fixed term, ISA, offshore, corporate and institutional deposits, with inflows, outflows and interest cost tied to the rate path.

Treasury and Funding

Ten securities holdings (gilts, supranationals, covered bonds, RMBS and ABS by liquidity level) and central bank cash. Wholesale funding across MREL senior bonds, TFSME, repo, Tier 2 and AT1, plus a balancing line that holds cash at its minimum.

Credit, Costs and RWA

ECL rate, charge and stock across eight portfolios, operating costs, and risk-weighted assets by portfolio.

Reg and Cap

CET1 build with deductions, AT1, Tier 2 and MREL. CET1, Tier 1, total capital and leverage ratios against Pillar 1, Pillar 2A, conservation, countercyclical, PRA and risk appetite buffers. LCR and NSFR.

BS, PL and CashFlow

Monthly balance sheet, P&L from interest income through retained profit, and a cash flow statement that ties to balance sheet cash in every month.

KPI, Analysis, CFO_Dashboard and Summary

Profitability, NIM, balance sheet, asset quality, capital, efficiency and valuation KPIs, charts, and an executive summary showing headroom to risk appetite and regulatory minimums.

Sensitivity and Audit

Rate, growth, ECL and cost sensitivities, and 23 integrity checks on the Audit tab, all reading PASS on the demo data.

What makes this different

Single source of truth

Every module reads one timeline and one Toggle tab. Switch between Base, Upside and Downside once and rates, volumes, credit losses, deposits and costs move together across all 32 tabs.

No circular references

Cash income is earned on the prior month-end balance and a balancing funding line holds cash at its minimum, so the plan closes every month with iterative calculation switched off.

23 integrity checks

The balance sheet balances, cash flow ties to balance sheet cash in all 60 months, interest and ECL tie between the engines and the P&L, and the capital hierarchy holds. All on one Audit tab.

Real bank P&L structure

NII, fees, cost of funds, opex, provisions, tax - the standard FP&A layout, not a generic three-statement.

Hedging and liquidity in the plan

Fixed-rate lending carries a hedge ratio and swap P&L into hedged NII, and LCR and NSFR are projected alongside the capital ratios.

Frequently asked questions

How long to set up the first run?

Most of the work is populating the eight input tabs with your bank's balances, rates and growth assumptions. The free sample shows every tab with real results on demo data, and its PL tab keeps live formulas, so you can see the structure before you start.

Does it cover stress testing?

Yes, at plan level. The Downside scenario cuts rates, raises ECL and slows growth, and the Sensitivity tab flexes rates, lending growth, ECL and costs. For full ICAAP or CCAR-grade stress testing, see our separate Bank Stress Test Model.

Does it cover ECL / provisions?

Provision rate is a flexible input. For full PD/LGD/EAD or stage migration, see our separate IFRS 9 / CECL model.
