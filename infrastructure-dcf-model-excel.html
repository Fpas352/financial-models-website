Infrastructure DCF Model

Value three infrastructure assets on their own revenue logic over 30 years: a toll road on traffic and tariff, a wind farm on capacity, availability and power price, and a water utility on its regulated asset base. Then size the debt to a target DSCR and test the covenants each year.

The problem it solves

Infrastructure assets do not earn money the same way. A toll road carries volume risk, a wind farm carries resource and price risk, and a regulated utility earns a return on its asset base. A generic DCF treats them alike and misses what drives each one.

This model gives each asset its own revenue build, then runs common schedules for opex, capex and RAB, debt, tax and free cash flow, so the three can be compared on one basis.

Who it's for

Infrastructure investors and fund analysts. Project finance and infrastructure lending teams sizing debt. Corporate finance advisers and students learning how regulated and contracted assets are valued.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

WACC, risk-free rate, inflation, tax and concession period; for each asset its capex, operating assumptions, revenue drivers, senior debt, rate, tenor and target DSCR. Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

REVENUE_MODEL

30-year revenue: toll traffic and tariff; wind capacity, capacity factor with degradation, availability, curtailment and power price; water RAB-based allowed revenue plus connections.

OPERATING_COSTS

Fixed and variable opex, insurance, management fees and lifecycle maintenance by asset, with EBITDA and margin.

CAPEX_RAB

Capex and the RAB roll-forward for each asset: opening, additions, depreciation and closing, with the return on RAB.

DEBT_SCHEDULE

Senior debt by asset: interest, amortisation, debt service, DSCR, LLCR, PLCR and DSRA, plus interest cover, leverage and debt to RAB.

TAX

Taxable income after tax depreciation and interest, losses carried forward, tax paid and the tax shield from debt.

FREE_CASH_FLOW

Pre-debt FCFF and post-debt FCFE by asset, with yields and cash-on-cash returns.

RETURNS_ANALYSIS

FCFF and FCFE totals, NPV at WACC, equity multiple, payback, average DSCR and gearing, and NPV at discount rates from 5% to 12%.

DEBT_SIZING

Maximum debt from CFADS and the target DSCR, headroom against the debt assumed, and a gearing table for the toll road from 50% to 80%.

COVENANT_DASHBOARD

DSCR, LLCR and PLCR against their minimums each year, the distribution lock-up test and distributions.

SCENARIO_ANALYSIS

All four scenarios for each asset side by side, with variance against the base case.

DASHBOARD

Portfolio NPV, equity multiple and average DSCR, with chart data for revenue, DSCR, FCFE, RAB and capital structure.

SENSITIVITY

Seven two-way tables, including portfolio NPV by volume growth and discount rate, and wind farm NPV by capacity factor and power price.

CHECKS

64 integrity checks, including the RAB and debt roll-forwards and cross-sheet consistency of revenue, EBITDA, debt service and capex for each asset.

What makes it different

Revenue built the way each asset earns

Traffic times tariff, generation times price after availability and curtailment, and allowed return on RAB plus depreciation for the utility.

Debt sized to cash flow

Maximum debt comes from CFADS and a target DSCR using an annuity formula, with headroom shown against the debt assumed.

Covenants every year

DSCR minimum 1.20x, LLCR 1.10x, PLCR 1.15x and a distribution lock-up below 1.15x DSCR, tested across the projection.

Tax that carries losses

Tax depreciation, interest deductibility and losses carried forward, so early-year tax reflects the capital structure.

Seven sensitivity tables

From discount rate and volume growth to capacity factor and power price, and DSCR target against interest rate for debt capacity.

Frequently asked questions

Which currency is it in?

The demo is in US dollars, millions. All values are inputs, so any currency works if you are consistent.

What does it not do?

There is no construction phase: operation is assumed from the start. LLCR and PLCR are DSCR-based approximations, tax depreciation uses a 150% accelerated proxy, and working capital is a percentage of the change in revenue. Returns are reported as NPV, equity multiple and payback rather than IRR.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo portfolio.
