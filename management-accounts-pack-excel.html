Management Accounts & Monthly Reporting Pack

A monthly reporting pack for a six-division group: P&L for the month and year to date against budget and prior year, divisional drill-downs, balance sheet, cash flow, revenue analysis, a 20-KPI scorecard with RAG status, an EBITDA bridge and a full-year forecast.

The problem it solves

The monthly pack is the same job every month: pull actuals, line them up against budget and last year, explain the bridge, refresh the KPIs and get it to the board on time. Rebuilding it each month is where the errors and the late nights come from.

This model gives the pack a fixed structure. Set the reporting month once and every budget and prior-year comparison picks up that month, year-to-date totals accumulate to it, and the full-year forecast combines the actual months with the budget for the rest.

Who it's for

Financial controllers and management accountants producing the monthly pack. FP&A teams reporting to ExCo and the board. Finance directors who want a consistent structure month after month.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Reporting year and month, general assumptions, monthly budget and prior year by division and line, the balance sheet budget, and KPI targets and RAG thresholds.

PL_MONTH

Consolidated P&L for the month: actual, budget and prior year, with variances in pounds and percent.

PL_YTD

Year-to-date P&L against budget and prior year, full-year budget and forecast, and a monthly trend for every revenue line by division.

BALANCE_SHEET

Balance sheet against budget and prior year, with a balance check, working capital and net debt.

CASHFLOW

Operating, investing and financing cash flows against budget and prior year, the cash bridge and monthly detail.

DIVISIONAL

P&L for each of six divisions, actual against budget, with contribution by division and cost by line and division.

REVENUE_ANALYSIS

Revenue by product, customer segment and geography, volume and price decomposition, pipeline and a 12-month trend.

KPI_SCORECARD

20 financial, operational and strategic KPIs against target and prior year, with trend and RAG status.

WATERFALL

EBITDA bridge from budget to actual by revenue and cost line, for the month and year to date.

FORECAST

Each month flagged actual or forecast from the reporting month, with the full-year forecast against budget and divisional detail.

DASHBOARD

Chart data for the P&L trend, divisional contribution, the EBITDA bridge, cash flow, key ratios and headcount.

SENSITIVITY

Full-year EBITDA by revenue growth and margin, net income by interest and tax rate, named stress cases, and divisional EBITDA sensitivity.

CHECKS

50 integrity checks, including the balance sheet balancing, the cash bridge closing, and the full-year forecast equal to actual plus remaining budget.

What makes it different

Frequently asked questions

What does it not do?

The full-year forecast is year-to-date actuals plus the remaining budget, not a driver-based reforecast. Balance sheet, cash flow, revenue analysis, KPI actuals and the EBITDA bridge are entered on their own tabs rather than derived from the P&L, and the pack reports one month at a time.

Is it set up for a bank?

The demo is a financial services group with six divisions, from Retail Banking to Digital Services, and banking KPIs such as NIM, CET1 and LCR. The P&L structure of revenue, direct costs and operating expenses works for other businesses if you relabel the lines.

How is it different from the Budget vs Actual Variance Model?

That model goes line by line through 40 revenue and cost lines in eight departments for the whole year. This one is the monthly pack: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, KPIs and the bridge for one reporting month.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
