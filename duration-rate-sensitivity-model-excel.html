Duration & Rate Sensitivity Model

Measure interest rate risk on a 20-bond portfolio and the funding behind it: a bootstrapped zero curve, Macaulay and modified duration, DV01, convexity and key-rate duration, repricing across 17 shocks, a duration gap against six liability lines, NII by repricing bucket and EVE under six shock scenarios.

The problem it solves

Duration is the first number anyone asks for on a fixed income book and the one most often quoted without its context. A portfolio duration means little without the liabilities it funds, the convexity that bends it, and the point on the curve where the exposure sits.

This model puts those together: duration and convexity bond by bond, key-rate durations across eight tenors, a duration and BPV gap against the liability profile, and the effect of the same rate moves on net interest income and economic value of equity.

Who it's for

ALM, treasury and market risk teams at banks and building societies. Investment teams managing bond portfolios against liabilities. CFA and FRM candidates and finance students who want duration and convexity worked through on real cash flows.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario selector, a 20-bond portfolio (face value, coupon, maturity, frequency, yield and spread), a 15-point par yield curve by scenario, shock levels, key-rate tenors, a six-line liability profile and the NII repricing profile.

YIELD_CURVE

Zero rates bootstrapped from par yields, discount factors, forward rates and an interpolated quarterly curve.

BOND_VALUATION

Cash flow discounting for each of the 20 bonds, with clean price and accrued interest.

MACAULAY_DURATION

Period-by-period PV weights and Macaulay duration per bond, and the portfolio total.

MODIFIED_DURATION

Modified duration, dollar duration and DV01 per bond, and by maturity bucket.

CONVEXITY

Convexity per bond from the full cash flow schedule, and the portfolio figure.

KEY_RATE_DURATION

Duration allocated across eight key rates from 6 months to 30 years, by bond and for the portfolio.

PRICE_SENSITIVITY

Value change for each bond and the portfolio across 17 shocks from -200 to +200 bp, with the estimate from duration alone beside the estimate including convexity.

NII_SENSITIVITY

Repricing gap across nine buckets from overnight to over five years, and the NII impact of eight parallel shocks plus a steepener and a flattener.

IMMUNISATION

Asset and liability duration, BPV and convexity, the duration and BPV gaps, equity at risk at +100 and +200 bp, and the adjustment needed to close the gap.

EVE

Economic value of equity under parallel +200 and -200 bp, steepener, flattener, short up and short down, with the worst case and the contribution by bond.

DASHBOARD

Key risk metrics and six charts.

SENSITIVITY

Grids of price change by duration and yield move, convexity benefit for a +200 bp shock, and NII impact by gap size and shock.

CHECKS

55 integrity checks, including discount factors decreasing, modified duration below Macaulay for every bond and key-rate durations summing to total duration.

What makes it different

Assets and liabilities together

A six-line liability profile sits beside the bond portfolio, so the model measures the duration gap, BPV gap and equity at risk, not just portfolio duration.

Key-rate duration

Exposure split across eight tenors from 6 months to 30 years shows where on the curve the risk sits.

Convexity shown, not assumed away

PRICE_SENSITIVITY sets the estimate from duration alone beside the estimate including convexity across 17 shocks, so you can see where the straight-line estimate breaks down.

Earnings and value

The same rate moves run through NII by repricing bucket and through EVE under six shock shapes, so the earnings view and the economic value view sit in one workbook.

A bootstrapped zero curve

Zero rates are bootstrapped from 15 par yields, with forward rates derived from them.

Frequently asked questions

What does it not do?

Each bond is discounted at its own yield to maturity rather than off the zero curve. Key-rate durations are allocated in proportion to maturity proximity rather than by bumping each tenor, and price and EVE changes use the duration and convexity approximation rather than full repricing.

How is it different from the IRRBB Model?

The IRRBB Model implements the regulatory framework: 19 Basel buckets, the six prescribed shocks, deposit slotting and the outlier tests. This model measures duration, convexity and rate sensitivity for a bond portfolio and its funding.

Can I use my own portfolio?

Yes. The 20 positions, the par yield curve and the liability profile are all inputs on INPUTS.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo portfolio, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
