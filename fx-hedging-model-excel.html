FX Hedging & Currency Risk Model

Manage a sterling company's exposure to six currencies over 60 months: net exposure by currency, forward curves, ten FX forwards and five cross-currency swaps marked to market, effectiveness testing, the P&L and OCI impact, a hedge ratio recommendation and FX value at risk.

The problem it solves

Foreign currency revenue and costs rarely net off neatly, and the hedge book built against them drifts as exposures change. Treasury teams end up answering the board's questions (how much is hedged, what it is worth today, whether it still qualifies, what risk is left) from separate spreadsheets.

This model holds the exposure, the hedges and the answers in one place. Each forward and swap is valued against its currency's forward curve every month, tested against the 80% to 125% dollar-offset range, and its mark-to-market is split between P&L and the OCI reserve.

Who it's for

Corporate treasury teams and group finance at companies with foreign currency revenue or costs. Finance directors who report hedge positions to the board or audit committee. Treasury analysts and students learning how a hedge programme is valued and tested.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario selector, spot rates, monthly forward points and volatilities for six pairs, monthly revenue and cost exposures, hedge policy limits, interest rate differentials and VaR parameters.

EXPOSURE

Revenue, cost and net exposure by currency for 60 months, with translation exposure and the cumulative position.

FORWARD_CURVES

Outright forward rates built from spot plus cumulative forward points, with discount factors, for each pair.

HEDGE_PORTFOLIO

Ten FX forwards: notional, contracted and current forward rate, mark-to-market, settlement P&L and hedge ratio contribution.

SWAP_PORTFOLIO

Five cross-currency swaps with quarterly leg payments, net settlement, NPV and principal exchange at maturity.

EFFECTIVENESS

Prospective and retrospective dollar-offset tests for all 15 instruments, with a pass rate.

MTM_VALUATION

Combined mark-to-market of forwards and swaps by month, split into realised and unrealised.

PL_IMPACT

Unhedged FX gains and losses, the effective portion to OCI, the ineffective portion to P&L, and the OCI reserve roll-forward.

HEDGE_STRATEGY

For each currency, expected P&L, volatility and cost of hedging at hedge ratios from 0% to 100%, and the recommended ratio beside the policy minimum and maximum.

VaR_FX

Parametric VaR at 95% and 99% over a 10-day holding period by currency, undiversified and diversified totals, VaR after hedging and each currency's contribution.

DASHBOARD

Key metrics and chart data for exposure, hedge ratios, MTM, P&L, effectiveness and VaR.

SENSITIVITY

Four two-way tables: P&L by spot shock and hedge ratio, VaR by volatility and exposure, hedging cost by forward points and tenor.

CHECKS

70 integrity checks, including forward equal to spot plus points for each pair and the OCI reserve roll-forward.

What makes it different

Exposure and hedges side by side

Net exposure by currency and month sits next to the forwards and swaps placed against it, so the hedge ratio is measured against the real position.

Forwards and cross-currency swaps

Ten forwards and five swaps, each valued monthly over the 60-month horizon.

Every instrument tested

Prospective and retrospective dollar-offset tests against the 80% to 125% range, instrument by instrument, with a pass rate.

OCI and P&L separated

Hedge MTM is split into the effective portion for the OCI reserve and the ineffective portion for P&L, and the reserve rolls forward each month.

A hedge ratio you can defend

For each currency, volatility reduction against cost of hedging at five hedge ratios, with the ratio that gives the highest net benefit.

Frequently asked questions

What does it not do?

It does not model counterparty credit risk, margin requirements or basis risk. VaR is parametric, forwards are assumed to be physically settled and swaps net settled.

Which currencies are covered?

Sterling against USD, EUR, JPY, CHF, AUD and CNY. Spot rates, forward points, volatilities and exposures are all inputs, so you can replace the demo values with your own.

What do the scenarios change?

The market and exposure inputs carry Base, Upside, Downside and Stress values, switched from one selector on INPUTS.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo company.
