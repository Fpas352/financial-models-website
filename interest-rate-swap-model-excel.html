The problem it solves

Treasury teams that hedge with swaps need to know three things quickly: what the book is worth, how much it moves for a basis point, and whether the hedges still work. Those answers usually sit in a vendor system that finance cannot open or check.

This model sets out the whole chain in formulas: curve construction, pricing, portfolio valuation, risk and hedge effectiveness, so every number can be traced and challenged.

Who it's for

Treasury and ALM teams at banks and corporates that hedge interest rate risk. Finance teams checking a counterparty's valuation. Analysts learning curve construction and swap pricing properly.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

Par curve by tenor, swap portfolio terms (notional, fixed rate, pay or receive), and Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

Par, zero and forward curves, with zero rates bootstrapped from par.

What makes it different

A curve you can see being built

Zero rates bootstrapped from market par rates and forwards derived by no-arbitrage, step by step.

DV01 by bump and reprice

Each swap is revalued under a one basis point shift, not approximated from duration.

A real portfolio, not one swap

Twenty positions, pay and receive, with MTM, DV01 and cash flows aggregated.

Hedge effectiveness and basis risk

Effectiveness as the ratio of hedge to exposure DV01, tested against the 80% to 125% band, with SONIA-swap basis risk.

Nine stress scenarios

Shifts to the short and long ends with non-parallel moves interpolated between them.

Frequently asked questions

Which curve does it use?

Any curve you enter: par swap rates by tenor on INPUTS. The defaults are sterling with SONIA-based basis.

What does it not do?

It assumes a flat volatility surface, the convexity adjustment is approximate, there is no CVA or DVA, and day counts are simplified for semi-annual periods.

Can I value my own swaps?

Yes. Each of the 20 portfolio rows takes its own notional, fixed rate, tenor and direction.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo portfolio, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
