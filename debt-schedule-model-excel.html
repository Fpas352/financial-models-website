Debt Schedule & Facility Management Model

Eight facilities, from a term loan and revolver to mezzanine, subordinated notes, leases and a shareholder loan, each on its own monthly schedule for five years. Consolidated into net debt and the weighted average cost of debt, with five covenants tested every month.

The problem it solves

A borrower's debt rarely sits in one instrument. Term loans amortise, the revolver is drawn and repaid, the bond is fixed and the mezzanine is not, and the covenant certificate needs all of it on one LTM basis every quarter.

This model gives every facility its own schedule, with interest on the prior month's closing balance so there is no circularity, then consolidates them and tests leverage, interest cover, DSCR, minimum liquidity and gearing against their limits each month.

Who it's for

Treasury and corporate finance teams managing a multi-facility capital structure. Finance directors preparing covenant compliance. Leveraged finance and private credit analysts monitoring a borrower.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

EBITDA and growth, opening cash, tax, a five-year base rate path, capex, working capital and covenant limits; for each facility: commitment, drawn amount, fixed or floating, spread or fixed rate, amortising or bullet, maturity, commitment and arrangement fees, prepayments and drawdowns. Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

FACILITY_1 to FACILITY_8

One monthly schedule per facility: drawdowns, scheduled repayment, voluntary prepayment, interest, commitment and arrangement fees, all-in cost, utilisation and effective rate.

CONSOLIDATED

All eight facilities combined: balances, interest, fees, debt service, net debt, weighted average cost of debt, weighted average maturity and utilisation.

COVENANTS

Leverage, interest cover, DSCR, minimum liquidity and gearing each month on an LTM basis, with headroom and a status for each.

MATURITY_PROFILE

Maturity ladder by facility, 12 and 24-month refinancing need, refinancing capacity and coverage, concentration and the maturity wall year.

INTEREST_ANALYSIS

Fixed against floating interest, effective rates, and the annual impact of base rate moves from -100 to +200 bps.

REFINANCING

Extend, reprice or replace the term loan and the revolver, with break costs, fees and the NPV saving of each option.

DASHBOARD

Headline KPIs and chart data for balances by facility and quarterly interest and fees.

SENSITIVITY

Interest cover by base rate and EBITDA, total interest by prepayment size and timing, and leverage by EBITDA growth and repayment rate.

CHECKS

100 integrity checks, including closing balance within commitment, no negative balances and the roll-forward on every facility.

What makes it different

Eight instruments, one structure

Senior term loan, revolver, secured bond, bilateral loan, mezzanine, subordinated notes, lease obligations and shareholder loan, each with its own rate basis, profile and fees.

No circularity

Interest runs on the prior month's closing balance, so the model needs no iterative calculation.

Five covenants, every month

Leverage, interest cover, DSCR, liquidity and gearing are tested on rolling 12-month figures, with headroom shown against each limit.

All-in cost, not just coupon

Commitment and arrangement fees sit beside interest, so each facility's effective rate and the weighted average cost of debt include them.

Refinancing with numbers

Three options for the term loan and the revolver, compared on total cost and NPV, before the maturity wall arrives.

Frequently asked questions

Which repayment profiles does it support?

Amortising, with equal quarterly principal repayments over the term, and bullet. Sculpted repayment is not implemented in this version.

Can I change the facility mix?

Yes. Each facility's commitment, rate basis, spread, profile, maturity and fees are inputs, so the eight slots can carry your own instruments.

What does it not do?

Day count is approximated as monthly twelfths for fixed and floating facilities. EBITDA grows at a constant rate, capex and working capital are held constant, and the cash flow is simplified. Refinancing options cover the term loan and the revolver.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo company.
