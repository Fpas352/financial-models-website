Credit Stress Test & Capital Model

Takes a loan tape of up to 1,000 loans from macro shock to impairments, risk-weighted assets and the CET1 ratio. Three years, three scenarios: baseline, adverse and severely adverse.

The problem it solves

Many credit stress tests apply a stressed loss rate to each segment and stop there. The supervisor's questions go further: how the loss arises, what the downgrades do to RWA as well as to capital, where the low point falls and how far it sits from the hurdle.

This model answers those from your own loan book. The stress moves every loan through a stressed transition matrix, so Stage 2 balances, provisions and risk weights all respond, and the results flow into an income statement and a capital plan.

Who it's for

Capital planning and stress testing teams at smaller banks and building societies preparing an ICAAP. CFOs and CROs who need a stress they can defend on their own book. Consultants and reviewers supporting ICAAP and supervisory review.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Severity dial, staging rules, starting capital, RWA outside the tape, income run-rate, hurdle rates and management actions.

SEGMENTS

Five segments with credit conversion factor, macro sensitivity, LGD parameters and standardised risk weights, including loan-to-value bands for mortgages.

SCENARIOS

Macro paths, the credit cycle index, collateral value paths and bank-level shocks for each scenario.

TM

Rating scale, transition matrix and the SICR matrix.

LIFETIME

Through-the-cycle cumulative PD by grade and horizon.

STRESSED

Stressed transition matrices by segment, scenario and year.

DIST

Grade distributions after one, two and three years.

FWD

Forward 12-month PD and lifetime uplift at each year-end.

LOANS

The loan tape (columns A to M) and the loan-level engine: probability of performing, Stage 2 and default, stressed LGD, provisions and RWA per loan.

CREDIT

Tape results by scenario, year and segment.

PNL

Income statement by scenario, without and with management actions.

CAPITAL

CET1, RWA, ratios and headroom by scenario.

BRIDGE

What moved the CET1 ratio over the three years.

REVERSE

Reverse stress test: how much more loss the capital position can absorb before the hurdle.

OUTPUTS

One-page summary and charts.

CHECKS

43 integrity checks.

What makes it different

The stress moves the whole matrix

Macro paths become a credit cycle index that shifts the transition matrix separately for each segment. Downgrades and defaults both rise, so Stage 2 migration and RWA inflation come through, not just losses.

Collateral drives LGD and risk weights

Collateral values follow their own path. Falling prices raise loss given default and push mortgages into higher loan-to-value risk-weight bands.

From loans to the CET1 ratio

Loan results feed an income statement and a capital plan: the CET1 ratio path, the low point against the hurdle, with and without management actions.

A bridge and a reverse stress test

BRIDGE shows what moved the ratio. REVERSE shows how many times larger losses could be before the hurdle is reached, and the cumulative loss rate that would get there.

A severity dial

One selector scales the adverse scenarios' credit cycle index and collateral falls, so you can show the board how sensitive capital is to the depth of the stress.

Frequently asked questions

Which capital approach does it use?

Standardised risk weights, with loan-to-value bands for mortgage segments. It does not calculate IRB risk weights.

How does it relate to the Stress Testing Model?

The Stress Testing Model works at portfolio level across the whole income statement. This model starts from the loan tape and gives a granular credit view with its own capital plan. They can be used together, or this one on its own for a credit-led stress.

What does it not do?

Annual steps over three years, expected values rather than simulated paths. No cures from default and no write-offs, so Stage 3 only grows. Tax losses are not carried forward. Under the static balance sheet option, maturing loans are replaced like for like.

How many loans can it take?

Up to 1,000 rows. Paste over the demo tape; blank rows return zero.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
