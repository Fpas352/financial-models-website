The problem it solves

When NIM misses budget, the first question at ALCO is why: balances, pricing, mix, the base rate or something else. Most finance teams rebuild that bridge by hand every month, and the pieces rarely add back to the total.

This model does the decomposition once, consistently, and reconciles it: volume, price, mix and the cross term add to the total NII variance, and the NIM walk runs from budget to actual in more than 16 steps.

Who it's for

Bank FP&A and finance business partners producing monthly NII and NIM commentary. ALCO secretaries and treasury teams. Finance directors who need the margin story in one page.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario (Base, Stress, Outperform, Custom), base rate paths, FTP add-on, pass-through betas and RAG thresholds.

ACTUALS

Actual monthly average balances, rates and NII by product.

BUDGET

Budget balances, rates and NII in the same structure.

VPM_ANALYSIS

Volume, price and mix variance for every product, with subtotals by category and a group total.

RATE_ANALYSIS

Book composition, new business and the monthly base rate impact, with a period selector.

NIM_WALK

The bridge from budget NIM to actual NIM in more than 16 steps: base rate, customer spread, FTP, hedging and mix.

RATE_IMPACT

The base rate effect by segment, using configurable betas.

DASHBOARD

Executive KPIs, RAG status and narrative.

CHECKS

Reconciliation checks: the variance components must add to the total.

Frequently asked questions

What data do I need?

Monthly average balances and rates by product, for actuals and budget. The structure of both tabs is the same, so you paste in two blocks.

Which base rate does it assume?

It is set up for the Bank of England base rate with configurable betas, but the rate path is an input, so it works for any policy rate.

How is it different from the Deposit Pricing & NII Model?

That model projects deposit pricing and NII forward. This one explains the past: actuals against budget, decomposed.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
