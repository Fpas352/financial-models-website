PP&E / Fixed Assets Model (IAS 16)

A fixed asset register of 120 assets across six classes, with gross cost, accumulated depreciation and net book value rolled forward month by month for five years. Disposals with their gain or loss, a revaluation schedule, a five-year capex plan and the carrying amount reconciliation for the accounts.

The problem it solves

The fixed asset note is often built once a year from a register in one place and a depreciation run in another. When the auditor asks why closing NBV does not equal opening plus movements, the answer takes days.

This model keeps cost, depreciation, impairment and disposals in one monthly roll-forward per class, and DISCLOSURE checks that closing equals opening plus movements for every class, every year.

Who it's for

Financial controllers and fixed asset accountants. Finance teams preparing the PP&E note or a capex budget. FP&A teams who need depreciation and capex for a plan.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Start date, 60-month horizon, tax and inflation rates; then for each of six classes: useful life, method, residual value, annual capex budget, disposal rate, revaluation frequency and adjustment, and annual impairment rate. Disposal proceeds as a percentage of NBV. Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

ASSET_REGISTER

120 assets, 20 in each class, with acquisition date, cost, life, method, residual value, accumulated depreciation, NBV, location and status.

COST_ROLL

Monthly gross cost by class: opening, additions, reclassifications, disposals at cost, write-offs and closing, plus capital commitments.

DEPRECIATION

Monthly charge by class on cost less residual value over useful life, depreciation released on disposals, and impairment. Land is not depreciated.

NBV_SCHEDULE

Gross cost, accumulated depreciation, cumulative impairment and NBV by class, with NBV as a share of cost, average age and remaining useful life.

REVALUATION

Revaluation adjustment by class on year-end NBV, the surplus to reserve, and an annual revaluation schedule.

DISPOSALS

Cost and depreciation disposed, NBV at disposal, proceeds, gain or loss, and tax on the gain or loss, by class and month.

CAPEX_PLAN

Five-year capex by class, split into maintenance and growth, set against depreciation, and committed against uncommitted.

PL_IMPACT

Depreciation, impairment and disposal gains and losses by class, monthly: the EBITDA to EBIT bridge.

DISCLOSURE

Carrying amount reconciliation by class for each year, depreciation methods and useful lives, and capital commitments contracted and authorised.

DASHBOARD

NBV by class, depreciation trend, capex against depreciation, asset age, disposal gains and losses, and an NBV waterfall.

SENSITIVITY

Useful life moved by up to three years either way, residual value flexed, and a two-way grid of life against residual value.

CHECKS

53 integrity checks, including NBV equal to cost less depreciation, and depreciation and disposal results tying to the P&L, for every class.

What makes it different

One roll-forward, every class

Cost, accumulated depreciation and NBV move month by month for land, buildings, plant and machinery, vehicles, fixtures and fittings, and IT equipment.

Disposals with the gain or loss

Proceeds are set as a percentage of NBV, so each disposal produces a gain or loss and the tax on it, and both flow to PL_IMPACT.

Capex against depreciation

The capex plan splits spend into maintenance and growth and sets it against the depreciation charge, so you can see whether the asset base is being replaced.

The note reconciles itself

DISCLOSURE builds the carrying amount reconciliation for each year and checks that closing equals opening plus movements.

Scenarios on the judgement calls

Useful lives, residual values, capex budgets and impairment rates all move with the scenario selector.

Frequently asked questions

Which asset classes does it cover?

Six: land, buildings, plant and machinery, vehicles, fixtures and fittings, and IT equipment, with 20 assets in each on the register. Land is not depreciated.

Is it monthly?

Yes. 60 monthly periods, with annual totals on CAPEX_PLAN, DISCLOSURE and DASHBOARD.

What does it not do?

The monthly class schedules depreciate on a straight line. Revaluation is calculated on REVALUATION and shown as a memo line in the reconciliation, applied after the period close; it does not feed back into the depreciation charge. Impairment is an annual rate per class, not a recoverable amount test: for that, see the IAS 36 Impairment Model.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo company.
