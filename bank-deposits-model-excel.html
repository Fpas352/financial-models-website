Deposits Model

Model a deposit book of seven products over 60 months: balances, rates with deposit beta and lag, cost of funds, FTP margin, behavioural maturity and the book's contribution to the LCR and NSFR.

The problem it solves

Deposit pricing decides most of a bank's margin, and it rarely moves one for one with the base rate. How much of each rate change you pass on, and how quickly, is the assumption behind every NIM forecast and every ALCO pricing decision.

This model makes that assumption explicit per product, with a beta, a lag and a floor, and follows it through to cost of funds, FTP margin, liquidity value and P&L contribution.

Who it's for

Treasury, ALM and finance teams at banks and building societies. Savings and deposit product teams setting rates. ALCO members who need the margin and liquidity effect of a pricing decision in one place.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INSTRUCTIONS

Step-by-step guide and common uses: board reporting, stress, product launch, rate sensitivity, ALCO pack.

INPUTS

Base rate path, FTP rate, opening balances and flow rates, floors, betas and lags for each product, with a Base, Upside and Downside selector.

BOOK

Monthly balances for current accounts, instant access, 30 and 90-day notice, one and two-year fixed, and ISAs.

PRICING

Effective product rates from the floor, beta and lagged base rate, and interest expense on average balances.

BEHAVIOURAL

Behavioural maturity using stickiness, core deposits and decay rates.

LIQUIDITY

Each product's LCR outflows and NSFR available stable funding.

FTP

Funds transfer pricing credit and margin by product.

PL

Deposit book P&L contribution.

SENSITIVITY

NIM and contribution sensitivities.

OUTPUTS

Eight KPIs and six charts, board-ready.

CHECKS

27 integrity checks.

What makes it different

Beta, lag and floor per product

Effective rate = MAX(floor, floor + beta x (lagged base rate - floor base)). Each product has its own beta and its own lag in months, so instant access and fixed-term deposits respond differently.

Margin through FTP

Each product is credited at the FTP rate, so you can see which products earn their keep and which cost more than they are worth.

Liquidity value alongside margin

LCR outflow rates and NSFR ASF factors per product show what each pound of deposits is worth for liquidity, not just what it costs.

Behavioural maturity

Stickiness and core deposit assumptions shift contractual maturities to behavioural ones.

No circularity

Interest uses average balances, so the model calculates cleanly without iterative calculation switched on.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from the Deposit Pricing & NII Model?

The Deposit Pricing & NII Model focuses on pricing and net interest income. This model covers the whole deposit book: balances, pricing, behavioural maturity, liquidity value, FTP and P&L contribution.

Can I add or rename products?

Every balance, flow and pricing assumption is set per product on INPUTS, and the seven rows can be relabelled for your own range. Adding a product means extending the formulas on BOOK.

Which liquidity rules does it use?

Basel III LCR outflow rates and NSFR available stable funding factors as defaults, editable if your regulator sets different add-ons.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
