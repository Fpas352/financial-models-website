The problem it solves

Clients and investment committees do not stop at whether the portfolio beat the benchmark. They ask whether it was the sector bets or the stock picks, and whether the excess return was paid for with excess risk.

This model breaks active return into allocation, selection and interaction for every security and month, rolls it up by sector and region, and puts tracking error, information ratio, Sharpe, Sortino and VaR beside it.

Who it's for

Portfolio managers and performance analysts at asset and wealth managers. Investment consultants and fund selectors reviewing a manager. CFA candidates and students learning performance attribution.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario, risk-free rate, management fee and a scenario return multiplier; 40 holdings with sector, region, portfolio and benchmark weights; monthly portfolio, benchmark and factor returns.

PORTFOLIO_HOLDINGS

40 securities by 12 months: weights, price returns and dividend returns.

BENCHMARK

Benchmark weights, returns and contributions for the same 40 securities.

BRINSON_ATTRIBUTION

Allocation, selection and interaction for every security and month.

FACTOR_ATTRIBUTION

Market, size, value, profitability and investment factor contributions by month, and cumulative.

RISK_ANALYTICS

Returns, excess returns and the building blocks of volatility for each security and month.

SECTOR_ATTRIBUTION

Portfolio and benchmark weights, over and underweights and returns by sector and month.

GEOGRAPHIC_ATTRIBUTION

The same view across six regions.

PERFORMANCE_SUMMARY

Monthly gross and net returns against benchmark, then annualised alpha, tracking error, information ratio, Sharpe, Sortino, volatility, maximum drawdown and VaR at 95% and 99%.

RISK_DECOMPOSITION

Systematic against idiosyncratic risk by security, from beta and volatility.

DASHBOARD

Headline returns and ratios, monthly attribution, sector weights and factor exposures.

SENSITIVITY

Information ratio by alpha and tracking error, return by beta and market move, and allocation effect by weight deviation and return spread.

CHECKS

75 integrity checks, including portfolio and benchmark weights summing to 100% every month and the Brinson effects summing to the total.

What makes it different

Brinson at security level

Allocation = (wp - wb) × (rb - Rb), selection = wb × (rp - rb), interaction = (wp - wb) × (rp - rb), for each of 40 securities in each of 12 months.

Factors as well as bets

The Fama-French five-factor view separates return earned from market, size, value, profitability and investment exposure.

Sector and region roll-ups

The same decomposition aggregated by sector and across six regions, so the committee sees the bets it recognises.

Gross and net of fees

The management fee is applied monthly, so alpha is shown before and after it.

Risk next to return

Tracking error, information ratio, Sharpe, Sortino, maximum drawdown and VaR, so excess return is judged against the risk taken.

Frequently asked questions

Can I load my own portfolio?

Yes. Replace the 40 holdings, their weights and the monthly portfolio, benchmark and factor returns on INPUTS. The demo returns are simulated for illustration.

Which attribution methods does it use?

Brinson-Hood-Beebower (1986) for allocation, selection and interaction, and the Fama and French (2015) five-factor model.

What does it not do?

Factor loadings are estimated, not regression-based. VaR assumes normally distributed returns, the risk decomposition is a linear approximation and currency effects are approximated from input rates. It covers 40 securities over 12 monthly periods.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo portfolio.
