The problem it solves

Loans priced off a rate card or a competitor quote can look profitable and still destroy value once expected loss and the capital they consume are counted. Credit committees increasingly ask for the risk-adjusted return, not just the margin.

This model builds the all-in price from its components and measures the return on the capital each loan uses, so you can see which product and grade combinations create value and which do not.

Who it's for

Lending, pricing and credit teams at banks and specialist lenders. Finance business partners supporting credit committee. Treasury teams setting FTP.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Base rates, funding spreads, PD and LGD by grade, operating costs, capital requirements, volumes and grade mix, with Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

FUNDING_COST

FTP build-up: blended wholesale and retail funding, liquidity premium and HQLA buffer cost.

CREDIT_SPREAD

Expected loss plus capital cost plus a migration premium, by product, grade and year.

OPERATING_COST

Operating cost allocation by product.

PRICING_ENGINE

All-in price for 6 products x 7 grades x 10 years.

CAPITAL_ALLOC

Regulatory capital from standardised RWA and the full requirement stack, plus economic capital.

PROFITABILITY

RAROC, EVA and ROE by product and grade, against the hurdle rate.

PORTFOLIO

Portfolio-level aggregation.

RATE_SENSITIVITY

NII impact of parallel shifts from -200 to +200 bps.

DASHBOARD

Six charts and KPIs.

SENSITIVITY

Two-way data tables.

CHECKS

76 integrity checks.

What makes it different

Price built from its parts

Funding cost, credit spread, operating cost and margin, each on its own tab, so a committee can see what each loan has to cover.

Credit spread that includes capital

Expected loss, the cost of the economic capital the loan consumes at the target return, and a migration premium.

RAROC and EVA against the hurdle

Every product and grade combination is shown as value-creating or value-destroying.

Capital from the real requirement stack

Regulatory capital uses RWA times CET1 minimum, Pillar 2A and the buffers, with an economic capital multiplier on top.

A wide grid

Six products, seven risk grades and ten years: 420 price points, all from one set of inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Which products and grades does it cover?

Six loan products and seven risk grades, both editable on INPUTS, priced over ten annual periods.

What does it not do?

It runs in annual periods, PD and LGD are constant within a scenario with no term structure of default, and portfolio effects are additive with no correlation between products.

How is it different from the Lending Model?

The Lending Model projects a loan book's balances, P&L and capital. This model prices individual products and grades and measures their risk-adjusted return.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo bank, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
