The problem it solves

The LCR itself is one division. Getting it right is not: Level 2 and 2B caps applied after haircuts, the 75% inflow cap, the right run-off rate for each deposit class, and a projection that shows where the ratio is heading, not just where it stands today.

This model handles those mechanics and extends them into what liquidity teams need for an ILAAP and for ALCO: the NSFR, idiosyncratic and market-wide stress, a maturity ladder, a funding plan and intraday monitoring.

Who it's for

Treasury and liquidity risk teams at banks and building societies. Teams preparing an ILAAP or the liquidity section of an ALCO pack. Finance and risk professionals who need to model the LCR effect of balance sheet plans.

What makes it different

Caps applied correctly

Level 2 assets capped at 40% and Level 2B at 15% of HQLA after haircuts, and inflows capped at 75% of outflows. These are where hand-built LCR spreadsheets most often go wrong.

Projected, not just measured

Five years of monthly HQLA, outflows and inflows, so you can see the ratio respond to growth plans, deposit mix changes and maturing funding.

Three stress scenarios with survival

Idiosyncratic, market-wide and combined stress, each with a 90-day survival horizon. Parameters are inputs.

LCR and NSFR together

Short-term and structural liquidity in one workbook, from the same balance sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Which rules does it follow?

CRR2 and the Basel III LCR (BCBS 238), with HQLA haircuts and caps per the Delegated Regulation, NSFR factors per CRR2, and references to PRA SS24/15. Run-off rates are set at regulatory minimums; calibrate higher where your experience shows it.

Can I use it for regulatory submissions?

It is a projection and planning model. Regulatory returns need counterparty-level data and validation against your own systems; this model shows where the numbers are heading and why.

How is it different from the ILAAP Model?

The ILAAP Model frames the full internal liquidity adequacy assessment. This model is the calculation engine for the LCR and NSFR, with stress testing and a maturity ladder.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
