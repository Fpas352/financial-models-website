CECL Model (ASC 326)

The lifetime allowance for credit losses across eight loan segments. Choose WARM or PD-LGD for each segment, apply the nine interagency qualitative factors, and get the ACL, the reserve for unfunded commitments and the period roll-forward.

The problem it solves

Many community banks and credit unions run CECL either on a vendor platform they cannot see inside, or on a spreadsheet that has grown by accretion since adoption. Neither is easy to explain when the examiner asks how the forecast reverts or why a qualitative factor moved.

This model sets the methodology out in the open: the reasonable and supportable period, the reversion, the lookback, the Q-factors and the method choice are each an input you can see and change.

Who it's for

CFOs, controllers and ACL committees at community banks and credit unions. Finance teams preparing for audit or examination. Consultants and auditors who need a transparent benchmark for a client's allowance.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario, reasonable and supportable forecast period, reversion period, lookback, forecast scalars and scenario weights.

POOLS

Eight segments in Call Report terms: 1-4 family residential, home equity lines, owner-occupied CRE, non-owner-occupied CRE, C&I, construction and land, auto and other consumer. Balance, remaining term, prepayment, PD, LGD and method for each.

HIST_LOSS

Ten years of annual net charge-off rates and the lookback average.

QFACTORS

The nine interagency qualitative factors, by segment.

WARM

Balance run-off and the remaining-life loss estimate.

PD_LGD

Probability of default times loss given default on the projected balances.

ACL

Allowance by segment, coverage ratios and the reserve for unfunded commitments.

ROLLFWD

Allowance roll-forward and provision for the period.

SENSITIVITY

All three scenarios, a probability-weighted allowance and a scalar grid.

OUTPUTS

One-page summary and charts.

CHECKS

19 integrity checks, including that every remaining term is within the model horizon.

What makes it different

Two methods, chosen per segment

Run WARM where history is the best guide and PD-LGD where you have default and loss data. Both are calculated for every segment, so you can compare before you choose.

Forecast and reversion made explicit

A scalar on loss rates applies in full during the reasonable and supportable period, then reverts to the historical rate on a straight line over the reversion period. Both periods are inputs.

The nine interagency Q-factors

Adjustments by factor and by segment, laid out so the committee can see what each one adds to the allowance.

Unfunded commitments and the roll-forward

The reserve for unfunded commitments sits beside the ACL, and ROLLFWD reconciles opening to closing allowance and gives the provision for the period.

Scenario weighting

Three scenarios, a probability-weighted allowance and a scalar grid on SENSITIVITY.

Frequently asked questions

WARM or PD-LGD: which should I use?

WARM suits segments with stable loss history and limited default data. PD-LGD suits segments where you track defaults and recoveries. The model calculates both and lets you pick per segment.

Is it loan level?

No. It works on eight pooled segments, which is how most community banks estimate CECL. Balances amortise on a straight line with a constant prepayment rate.

How is this different from the IFRS 9 / CECL model?

That model is a PD/LGD/EAD engine with a switch between IFRS 9 and CECL. This one is built for ASC 326 only, with WARM, the interagency Q-factors, the unfunded commitment reserve and the roll-forward.

What does it not do?

Annual periods and remaining terms up to 15 years. Losses are applied to projected balances, so exposure is not reduced for earlier defaults, which is slightly conservative. It supports management judgement and audit, it does not replace them.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
