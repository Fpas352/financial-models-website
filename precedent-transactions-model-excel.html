Precedent Transactions Model

What have buyers actually paid? A database of up to 25 transactions with LTM and forward multiples, takeover premiums against undisturbed prices, announced synergies, screening, trends over time and an implied valuation for your target.

The problem it solves

Precedent multiples carry a control premium and often synergies, and they come from different points in the cycle. Applied without adjustment, they overstate what a target is worth today.

This model separates the pieces: premiums measured against undisturbed prices one day, one week and four weeks before announcement, synergies on their own tab, and time trends so you can see whether the deals you are citing come from a different market.

Who it's for

M&A and corporate finance advisors. Corporate development teams pricing an acquisition. Private equity associates benchmarking entry multiples.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Target financials, screening criteria and premium parameters, with Base, Upside and Downside scenarios.

DEAL_DATA

Up to 25 transactions across five sectors with full deal financials.

DEAL_METRICS

EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT and P/E on LTM, NTM and NTM+1 bases for every deal.

PREMIUM_ANALYSIS

Takeover premiums against undisturbed prices one day, one week and four weeks before announcement.

SYNERGY_ANALYSIS

Announced synergies and their effect on the price paid.

SCREENING

Include or exclude by date range, sector, deal size, and outliers beyond two standard deviations.

STATISTICS

Mean, median, quartiles, high and low, by sector and by year.

TIME_TRENDS

How multiples and premiums have moved over time.

IMPLIED_VALUATION

Selected multiples applied to the target: enterprise value, equity value and share price, with a football field.

DASHBOARD

Charts.

SENSITIVITY

Implied share price under different multiple and premium assumptions.

CHECKS

49 integrity checks.

What makes it different

Premiums measured properly

Against undisturbed share prices at three points before announcement, so leaks and run-ups do not distort the premium.

Synergies separated

Announced synergies on their own tab, so you can see how much of the multiple was the buyer's synergy rather than the target's value.

LTM and forward multiples

Every deal on trailing and two forward bases.

Time trends

Deals from different cycles shown as such, so you choose the relevant window rather than averaging across it.

A full equity bridge

Enterprise value = equity value + net debt + minorities + preferred, applied consistently to every deal.

Frequently asked questions

Can I load my own deals?

Yes. Replace the pre-loaded transactions on DEAL_DATA with your own set; screening, statistics and the implied valuation update automatically.

How many scenarios does it have?

Three: Base, Upside and Downside, switched from one cell on INPUTS.

How should I use the result?

As one input in a wider valuation alongside comparable companies and a DCF. The football field shows the ranges together.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo deal set, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
