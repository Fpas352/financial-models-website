ICAAP Model Excel Template

A five-year ICAAP capital model for UK banks: 20 quarters of RWA across ten asset classes, a full CET1, AT1 and Tier 2 build, Pillar 2A and 2B, combined buffers and MDA headroom, run through mild, moderate and severe stress.

Who it's for

Capital planning and finance teams at UK banks and building societies preparing or refreshing an ICAAP, and the consultancies that support them.

What's in the model

COVER and INSTRUCTIONS

Contents, version and a step-by-step guide to the first run.

INPUTS

One scenario selector (Base, Upside, Downside, Stress) and every assumption: opening capital position, CET1 components and deductions, AT1 and Tier 2 instruments, RWA drivers, stress parameters and Pillar 2A loadings.

RWA_PROJECTIONS

Credit RWA for ten Basel asset classes over 20 quarters, from exposure at default and risk weight. Market risk RWA from VaR and stressed VaR. Operational risk RWA under the Basic Indicator Approach.

CAPITAL_ADEQUACY

CET1 built up from share capital, reserves and profit, less deductions (goodwill, intangibles, DTA, pension assets, IRB shortfall and others). AT1 and Tier 2 rolled forward each quarter with issuance, calls and coupons.

STRESS_TESTING

Mild, moderate and severe scenarios. Credit losses by portfolio, market losses, NII impact, RWA uplift, operational, pension and conduct losses, net of tax relief, carried through to the stressed CET1 ratio.

ICAAP_CAPITAL_PLAN

CET1 generation quarter by quarter (profit, dividends, buybacks, AT1 coupons, OCI), the planned ratio path against target and minimum, and the AT1 and Tier 2 issuance schedule.

PILLAR2

Pillar 1, Pillar 2A add-ons (credit concentration, IRRBB, pension, operational and conduct), Pillar 2B as the PRA buffer, the total requirement stack and the ICAAP surplus or deficit.

BUFFERS

Conservation, countercyclical, O-SII and G-SII buffers, the MDA trigger ratio, headroom in basis points and £m, the distributable amount quartile, and headroom under each stress scenario.

RAF_LIMITS

Risk appetite metrics for capital, credit, liquidity, market and operational risk, set against their limits.

CONCENTRATION

Largest single-name exposures and a sector HHI, with an HHI-adjusted Pillar 2A figure to set against the input add-on.

IRRBB

Repricing gap and ladder by time band, EVE and NII sensitivity at plus and minus 200bps, and the outlier test.

DASHBOARD

CET1 under each stress scenario, RWA composition, capital stack waterfall, stress depletion and buffer utilisation charts.

SENSITIVITY

Four two-way tables, including CET1 ratio by credit loss rate and RWA growth, and headroom by dividend payout and organic capital generation.

CHECKS

109 integrity checks: capital hierarchy, RWA tie-outs and capital adequacy. All read PASS on the demo data.

NOTES and DISCLAIMER

Methodology notes and the model's scope.

What makes this different

One requirement stack

Pillar 1, Pillar 2A, the combined buffer and the PRA buffer are built up on one tab, so the MDA trigger and the ICAAP surplus come from the same numbers the capital plan uses.

Stress carried through to capital

Each scenario's losses, RWA uplift and NII impact land on CET1, so the low point against the MDA trigger shows quarter by quarter, alongside the base plan.

Five years, quarterly

Twenty quarters of RWA, capital and buffers, with AT1 and Tier 2 issuance, calls and coupons scheduled across the horizon.

Open and checked

Every formula is visible, there are no macros, and 109 integrity checks sit on one tab. Analytical and management models, not regulatory reporting or compliance tools.

Frequently asked questions

Which framework does it follow?

The UK PRA framework on Basel 3.1 terms: Pillar 1 minimums, Pillar 2A, the PRA buffer and the combined buffer. Buffer rates, risk weights and add-ons are all inputs, so they can be set to your own SREP outcome. It is an analytical and management model, not a regulatory reporting or compliance tool.

Does it cover ILAAP?

No. Liquidity sits in the separate ILAAP Model.

Is there a US equivalent?

Yes - our Bank Stress Test Model covers CCAR / DFAST for US BHCs.
