The problem it solves

Search for IRRBB and you find regulation, reporting templates and enterprise ALM systems. What you rarely find is the calculation itself, built and ready to run.

Smaller banks without an ALM system end up rebuilding the Basel standardised framework in a spreadsheet: the buckets, the shock shapes, the deposit slotting, the outlier tests. This model is that framework, already built, with every assumption on one inputs tab.

Who it's for

ALM and treasury teams at smaller banks and building societies. Risk teams preparing IRRBB reporting or the ICAAP interest rate risk section. ALCO secretaries who need the numbers on one page. Consultants and auditors checking a client's IRRBB figures.

What makes it different

All six prescribed shocks

Parallel up and down, short up and down, steepener and flattener. Short and long shocks decay exponentially with maturity, steepener and flattener are the Basel combinations, and a post-shock floor stops rates falling through a set lower bound. Defaults are sterling; every shock size is editable for your currency.

Non-maturity deposits slotted properly

Deposits are split into core and non-core using the stable share and pass-through rate, subject to the Basel caps. Non-core reprices overnight; core is spread out to twice its average life. The scenario selector flexes these assumptions, because they drive the answer.

Both outlier tests

Worst-case delta EVE and delta NII as a share of Tier 1, against thresholds you set (defaults: 15% for EVE, 5% for NII).

PV01 by bucket

Key-rate sensitivity shows where in the curve the risk sits, not just how big it is.

One page for ALCO

OUTPUTS puts the six EVE results, the NII result and the outlier status on a single printable page.

Frequently asked questions

Which rules is it built on?

The Basel standardised framework for IRRBB: 19 time buckets, six prescribed shocks and the supervisory outlier tests. Bucket boundaries, shock sizes and thresholds are inputs, so you can align them to your regulator's version.

Does it model option risk?

Behavioural optionality is captured through deposit slotting. Prepayment and early redemption are not modelled separately: reflect them in the buckets you enter. Automatic options such as caps and floors are not modelled.

Can it handle more than one currency?

One currency per copy. Run a copy for each material currency.

How is it different from the Duration & Rate Sensitivity Model?

That model measures duration and rate sensitivity for a portfolio. This one implements the regulatory IRRBB framework: Basel buckets, the six shocks, deposit slotting and the outlier tests.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
