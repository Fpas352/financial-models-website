Debt Amortisation & Repayment Model

Six tranches on six repayment profiles, month by month: two 30-year equal-payment loans, equal principal, bullet, balloon and a stepped sculpted profile. Compare them side by side, test a prepayment, price a refinancing and see the all-in cost once fees are counted.

The problem it solves

The same headline rate can cost very different amounts depending on how the loan repays. Borrowers comparing offers, and lenders explaining them, need the full schedule, not just the monthly payment.

This model runs every profile from the same inputs and puts total interest, weighted average life and the effective rate including fees next to each other, so the comparison is like for like.

Who it's for

Corporate treasury and finance teams comparing facility offers. Lenders and brokers explaining repayment structures. Property investors and finance students learning how amortisation works.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

For each tranche: principal, rate, term, frequency, start date, arrangement fee and annual fee; plus the balloon percentage, prepayment month and amount, and refinancing terms. Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

TRANCHE_1 to TRANCHE_6

Monthly schedule per tranche: opening balance, payment, interest, principal, closing balance, cumulative interest and principal, and percentage repaid.

COMPARISON

All six side by side: total interest, total payments, interest to principal, weighted average life, a simple effective rate, and outstanding balance at annual snapshots.

PREPAYMENT

A lump-sum prepayment on the first mortgage tranche, the estimated interest saving, and a grid of savings by amount and timing.

REFINANCING

Existing against new terms at a chosen month: payment saving, break cost and new fee, net saving and payback period, with 60 months of monthly comparison.

EFFECTIVE_RATE

All-in cost of borrowing by tranche, including arrangement and annual fees, with an annual cash flow summary for the IRR approximation.

DASHBOARD

Portfolio KPIs and chart data, including balance decline year by year.

SENSITIVITY

Total interest by rate and term, and interest saved by prepayment amount and timing.

CHECKS

75 integrity checks.

What makes it different

Six profiles, one set of inputs

Equal payment, equal principal, bullet, balloon and sculpted, so the difference you see is the structure, not the assumptions.

Sculpting you can read

The sculpted tranche repays 0.5% of the balance a month for the first 40% of the term, 1% up to 70%, then 2%, with the remainder at maturity.

Fees in the rate

EFFECTIVE_RATE adds arrangement and annual fees to interest, so the all-in cost of each tranche can be set against its nominal rate.

Prepay or refinance

Interest saved by a lump-sum prepayment, by amount and timing, and the payback period of a refinancing once break costs and new fees are paid.

No circularity

Interest is calculated on the prior period's closing balance.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from the Debt Schedule Model?

This model compares repayment structures for individual loans. The Debt Schedule Model runs a borrower's whole capital structure of eight facilities, consolidates them and tests covenants.

What does it not do?

Day count is 30/360 on monthly periods, with payments at month end. Prepayment savings are approximations, the sculpted tranche uses a stepped formula rather than a link to a cash flow forecast, and the IRR approximation on EFFECTIVE_RATE uses annual cash flows. Payment holidays, grace periods and tax effects are not modelled.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo portfolio.
