The problem it solves

Segment-level ECL models average away the loans that drive the allowance. When the auditor asks why one exposure moved to Stage 2, or why the allowance rose this quarter, a pool model cannot answer at loan level.

Enterprise IFRS 9 engines can answer, but they take months to implement and cost far more than a smaller lender needs to spend. This model sits between the two: loan-by-loan staging and measurement in a workbook your team can open, trace and defend.

Who it's for

Credit risk and impairment teams at smaller banks, building societies and specialist lenders. Finance teams producing IFRS 9 numbers without an enterprise engine. Auditors, advisors and model validators who need an independent loan-level benchmark.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario weights (three sets on a selector), staging rules, SICR thresholds and sensitivity settings.

SEGMENTS

Five segments: residential mortgages, SME, corporate, consumer loans and credit cards. Credit conversion factor, macro sensitivity, unsecured loss rate, collateral haircut and time to sale, LGD floor and management overlay for each.

MACRO

Macroeconomic paths for four scenarios, combined into a credit cycle index per scenario and year.

TM

Rating grades and the annual through-the-cycle transition matrix.

PD_CURVES

Cumulative PDs from compounding the matrix year after year, converted to hazard rates, then shifted for each scenario by the credit cycle index.

LOANS

The loan tape (columns A to O) and the loan-level engine: stage, 12-month and lifetime ECL per loan per scenario, probability-weighted.

STAGING

Stage mix, which trigger moved each loan, and past-due analysis.

ECL

Allowance by segment, by rating grade and by stage, with coverage ratios.

SCENARIOS

ECL under each scenario on its own and under each weighting set.

MOVEMENT

Stage transfers and the reconciliation of the opening to the closing allowance.

DISCLOSURE

IFRS 7 credit quality and loss allowance tables.

SENSITIVITY

Allowance against the SICR threshold, staging bounds and overlays.

OUTPUTS

One-page summary and charts.

CHECKS

36 integrity checks that turn red if anything stops reconciling.

What makes it different

Five staging triggers, loan by loan

Stage 3 on days past due or a default grade. Stage 2 on the past-due backstop, a relative and absolute rise in lifetime PD since origination, a downgrade of a set number of notches, watchlist, or forbearance. Loans at or better than the low-credit-risk grade are exempt from the PD test. STAGING shows which trigger fired.

PD curves you can trace

Lifetime PDs come from the transition matrix, not a typed-in table. A neutral economy (credit cycle index of zero) returns the through-the-cycle curve exactly, so the forward-looking adjustment is visible and separable.

Four scenarios, probability-weighted

Every loan is measured under each scenario and weighted. Switch between three weight sets to see how much of the allowance is driven by the downside.

Collateral-aware LGD

LGD applies a forced-sale haircut and time to sale to the collateral, a loss rate to the uncovered part, and a floor. Set per segment.

Built for the year-end pack

The movement table and IFRS 7 tables come straight from the loan-level results, so the disclosure ties to the engine without a manual bridge.

Frequently asked questions

How many loans can it take?

Up to 1,000 rows on the LOANS tab. Paste your tape over the demo loans; blank rows return zero. For a larger book, run it by portfolio or group similar loans into representative rows.

How is this different from the £299 IFRS 9 / CECL model?

That model works at segment level and switches between IFRS 9 and CECL. This one works loan by loan, with five staging triggers per loan, transition-matrix PD curves and IFRS 7 disclosure tables. Choose this one when you need to explain individual exposures.

What does it not do?

It runs in annual steps over a 20-year horizon. Exposure is bullet or straight-line amortising, LGD is constant over a loan's life within a scenario, and there is no cure or prepayment modelling. Prior-period stage and ECL are inputs, used for the movement table.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA, no hidden sheets. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live. The only difference is that inputs are locked, so you evaluate it on the demo loan book rather than your own.
