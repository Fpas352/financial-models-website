Credit Analysis Model

Underwrite and monitor up to 15 corporate borrowers in one workbook: five years of history and five of forecast, ratios, Altman Z-score, a composite rating, PD, LGD and expected loss, covenant headroom, stress and a structured credit decision.

The problem it solves

A credit paper needs the same analysis every time: the financials, the ratios, a rating, a probability of default, the loss if it goes wrong, the covenants and what happens under stress. Done by hand per borrower, it is slow and inconsistent across a portfolio.

This model runs that analysis consistently across 15 obligors and ends in a recommendation for each: approve, approve with conditions, or decline, priced by credit spread.

Who it's for

Corporate and commercial credit analysts at banks and private credit funds. Relationship and portfolio managers monitoring covenants. Students preparing for credit analyst roles.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Per-borrower financials, seniority, sector, facility size, tenor and collateral, with Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

FINANCIAL_ANALYSIS

Income statement history and forecast.

BALANCE_SHEET

Balance sheet over ten periods.

CASH_FLOW

Cash flow over ten periods.

RATIOS

Leverage, coverage, liquidity and profitability ratios.

CREDIT_SCORING

Altman Z-score with safe, grey and distress zones, and the Z-prime variant for private companies.

RATING_ANALYSIS

Composite rating from AAA to CCC, combining Z-score, leverage and coverage.

PROBABILITY_DEFAULT

One-year PD by rating, cumulative five-year PD via a migration matrix, and a through-the-cycle to point-in-time adjustment.

EXPOSURE_LGD

LGD by seniority with a collateral haircut and floor, and expected loss.

COVENANT_MONITORING

Net debt to EBITDA, interest cover and minimum liquidity headroom.

STRESS_TESTING

Revenue and margin shocks and a rate rise, re-running every ratio and rating.

CREDIT_DECISION

Approve, approve with conditions or decline, priced by credit spread, per obligor.

DASHBOARD

Portfolio-level charts.

SENSITIVITY

Two-way sensitivity tables.

CHECKS

48 integrity checks.

What makes it different

A portfolio, not one borrower

Fifteen obligors analysed the same way, so ratings and decisions are consistent and comparable.

A rating built from evidence

Z-score, leverage and coverage each imply a rating; the composite averages them rather than relying on a single factor.

From rating to expected loss

One-year and cumulative PD, LGD by seniority after collateral, and expected loss on the facility.

Covenants with headroom

Leverage, interest cover and liquidity tested every period, so a breach shows before it happens.

Stress that re-rates

Shocks flow through the financials, so ratios, ratings and decisions all respond.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for private companies?

Yes. Use the Z-prime variant, which replaces market value of equity with book value.

What does it not do?

Collateral values are static, there is no wrong-way risk modelling, and portfolio correlation between obligors is assumed to be zero. The distance-to-default proxy is simplified.

How often should I update it?

With each set of audited annual financials. Inputs are annual.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo portfolio, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
