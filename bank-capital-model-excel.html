The problem it solves

Capital adequacy is a chain: instruments less deductions give CET1, RWA set the denominator, Pillar 1, Pillar 2A and the combined buffer stack up to the MDA trigger. A break anywhere in that chain is how a bank ends up surprised by its own headroom.

This model sets the whole chain out on separate, traceable tabs and projects it forward, so capital planning, stress and the board view all come from the same numbers.

Who it's for

Capital management, finance and treasury teams at banks and building societies. Teams preparing the ICAAP capital plan or board capital papers. Analysts and consultants assessing a bank's capital position.

What makes it different

Deductions done properly

CET1 is built from qualifying instruments less the full list of regulatory deductions, each on its own line, so the capital number can be traced to the balance sheet.

The MDA trigger, explicit

Pillar 1 CET1, Pillar 2A CET1 and the combined buffer requirement add up to the MDA trigger, shown against the projected CET1 ratio month by month.

Pillar 2 and the ICAAP surplus

Pillar 2A and 2B sit in the stack, so the surplus over total requirement is visible, not just the surplus over Pillar 1.

Stress on both sides of the ratio

Each stress scenario applies after-tax losses to capital and an uplift to RWA, so the ratio is hit from both directions.

Plan and board view from one source

The five-year capital plan and the dashboard read from the same engine as the ratios.

Frequently asked questions

Which rules is it based on?

The Basel III capital framework as applied by the PRA in the UK, with references to the PRA Rulebook and the onshored CRR. Rates such as buffers and Pillar 2A are inputs.

What does it not do?

Risk weights are static, with no credit migration. Operational risk uses the Basic Indicator Approach. Counterparty credit risk under SA-CCR and MREL are not included. For the Basel 3.1 RWA changes, see the Basel IV RWA Calculator.

How does it relate to the ICAAP Model?

The ICAAP Model frames the whole internal capital adequacy assessment. This model is the capital engine: the stack, RWA, buffers, Pillar 2, stress and the capital plan.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
