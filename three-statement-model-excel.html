The problem it solves

The test of a three-statement model is simple: does the balance sheet balance and does cash tie, every period, in every scenario? Many templates pass in the base case and break the moment you change an assumption.

This model is built to stay tied: interest on prior-period balances so there is no circularity, every schedule reconciled, and integrity checks on every period.

Who it's for

FP&A teams building a budget or long-range plan. Founders and finance leads preparing investor or lender forecasts. Analysts and students learning how the three statements link.

Frequently asked questions

Can I adapt it to my business?

Yes. Segment names, revenue drivers and every cost and balance sheet assumption are inputs. The demo is a diversified technology company.

What does it not include?

Share issuance and buybacks, seasonality, covenant breach mechanics, foreign exchange and acquisitions beyond PP&E. Deferred tax is simplified, and no tax credit is taken on losses.

Is it suitable for a bank?

No. Banks need a balance-sheet-driven model with NII, provisions and capital: see the Bank Long-Term Plan Model.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo company, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
