Trading Comparables Model

Value a target against a peer group of up to 30 companies across five sectors: more than 15 multiples, screening with outlier removal, peer statistics, regression of multiples on fundamentals, and an implied valuation range.

Buy Now - £199    Download the Free Sample

The problem it solves

A comps table is easy to build and easy to get wrong. The peer set drifts, one outlier drags the mean, and nobody can say why the target deserves the median rather than the top quartile.

This model makes those judgements explicit: screening rules you set, outliers removed by a standard-deviation test you control, statistics on what is left, and a regression that ties multiples to growth and margins so a premium or discount has a reason.

Who it's for

Investment banking and corporate development analysts. Equity research and private equity associates. Finance teams valuing a business or a stake for a transaction or an impairment test.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Target company financials, screening criteria, outlier threshold, control premium, liquidity discount, size and country risk premiums, with Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

COMP_DATA

Up to 30 comparable companies with eight years of data: three historical, the current year and four forecast.

VALUATION_METRICS

More than 15 multiples, including EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, P/E and PEG, on trailing and forward bases.

GROWTH_MARGINS

Growth rates, margins and returns: ROIC, ROE and ROA.

SCREENING

Inclusion and exclusion by market cap and margin, and outlier detection by standard deviations from the mean.

STATISTICS

Mean, median and percentiles for each multiple across the screened peers.

IMPLIED_VALUATION

Peer multiples applied to the target: implied enterprise value, equity value and share price.

SECTOR_ANALYSIS

Sector-level averages and premiums across five sectors.

REGRESSION

OLS regression of multiples on operating metrics.

DASHBOARD

Charts and visual summary.

SENSITIVITY

Implied share price across a range of assumptions.

CHECKS

58 integrity checks.

What makes it different

Screening you can defend

Peers are included or excluded by rules you set on INPUTS, not by hand, and outliers are removed by a standard-deviation threshold.

Statistics on the clean set

Mean, median and percentiles are calculated after screening, so one outlier cannot move the answer.

A reason for the premium

Regression of multiples on growth and margins shows what the market pays for fundamentals, so the target's position in the range can be argued.

Adjustments in the open

Control premium, liquidity discount, size premium and country risk are explicit inputs, not buried in a chosen multiple.

Trailing and forward

Multiples on historical and forecast years side by side.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own peer group?

Yes. COMP_DATA takes up to 30 companies; replace the pre-loaded data with your own peers and their financials.

Which currency is it in?

The demo data is in US dollars, millions. All values are inputs, so any currency works if you are consistent.

How is it different from the Multi-Method Valuations Model?

This model goes deep on one method: comparable companies. The Multi-Method model runs six methods side by side, with comps as one of them.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo peer set.
