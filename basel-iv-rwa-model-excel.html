The problem it solves

The Basel III final reforms (Basel IV, or Basel 3.1 in the UK) change almost every RWA calculation at once: a new standardised approach, constraints on IRB, the output floor, the SMA for operational risk and a revised CVA framework. Most banks need to see the combined effect on their ratios before the rules bite, not one component at a time.

This calculator brings all of it into one place, so you can see where the floor binds, what moving a portfolio from SA to IRB is worth, and how the capital ratios respond.

Who it's for

Capital management and regulatory reporting teams preparing for Basel 3.1 and CRR3. Banks on or moving to IRB that need to understand the output floor. Consultants and analysts sizing the RWA impact of the reforms.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INSTRUCTIONS

Methodology, scenario definitions and how to run the model.

INPUTS

Every assumption, with a scenario selector across Base, Upside, Downside and Stress, and the output floor percentage set by quarter.

CREDIT_RWA_SA

Standardised approach across 12 exposure classes, from sovereigns and banks to corporates, retail and real estate: EAD, risk weight, RWA, density and capital requirement.

What makes it different

The output floor, class by class

The floor is calculated in total and for each exposure class, so you can see which portfolios drive the add-on rather than just its size.

A phase-in you control

The floor percentage is an input for each of the 20 quarters, so you can follow the Basel timetable or your own regulator's transitional path.

The real IRB formula

IRB capital uses the Vasicek single-factor model with asset correlation, the 99.9% confidence level and the maturity adjustment, not a lookup of indicative risk weights.

SA to IRB migration

MIGRATION shows the RWA effect of moving exposure classes between approaches, once the floor is taken into account.

All four risk types together

Credit, market, operational and CVA RWA roll into one summary and one set of ratios, under four scenarios.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Basel 3.1 / CRR3?

It implements the Basel III final reforms as published by the Basel Committee in December 2017, which Basel 3.1 in the UK and CRR3 in the EU are based on. Local variations, such as specific risk weights and the transitional floor path, are inputs you can align to your rules.

What does it not cover?

Securitisation exposures and counterparty credit risk under SA-CCR are not included. CVA uses the basic approach only, and the market risk standardised approach uses simplified risk weights.

How is it different from the Capital / Regulatory Model?

The Capital Model plans the capital base, buffers, Pillar 2 and the five-year capital plan on the current rules. This calculator goes deep on RWA under the final reforms: the new standardised approach, IRB, the output floor, FRTB, SMA and CVA.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo bank.
