The problem it solves

A bond portfolio is easy to list and harder to explain. Which positions carry the rate risk, how much of the yield is credit, and what a 100 basis point move or a spread widening does to the book are questions that usually get answered one bond at a time.

This model answers them across the whole portfolio at once. Every bond's duration, DV01 and convexity feed a scenario engine that reprices all 30 positions under parallel shifts, a steepener, a flattener, a credit spread widening and a default case, and totals the P&L by scenario.

Who it's for

Fixed income analysts and portfolio managers. Treasury and investment teams at banks, building societies and insurers holding gilt and credit portfolios. Students and graduates learning bond maths on a realistic book.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

The reference bond's terms, a 13-point benchmark curve and a swap curve, the 30-bond portfolio (par, coupon, frequency, maturity, sector, rating and market price) and stress parameters.

BOND_PRICING

Reference bond engine: cash flow schedule up to 60 semi-annual periods, clean price, accrued interest, dirty price, current yield, spread to benchmark and a price-yield table.

YIELD_ANALYSIS

Each bond's yield split into risk-free rate, credit spread, I-spread, liquidity premium, carry and roll-down.

DURATION

Macaulay and modified duration, DV01 and convexity for every bond, with a period-by-period workings block for each.

PORTFOLIO

Market value, weights and contributions to yield, duration and convexity; sector, rating and maturity breakdowns; aggregate portfolio cash flows.

TOTAL_RETURN

Ten-year projection of income, price and reinvestment return by bond and for the portfolio, against a benchmark return.

SCENARIOS

Nine scenarios across all 30 bonds: parallel +50, +100, +200, -50 and -100 bp, steepener, flattener, credit spread +100 bp and a default case at the assumed recovery rate.

DASHBOARD

Key portfolio metrics and seven charts.

SENSITIVITY

Portfolio value for yield shifts from -200 to +200 bp, each bond's impact from a +100 bp shift, and a credit spread by duration grid.

CHECKS

15 integrity checks, including clean price plus accrued equal to dirty price, weights summing to 100% and modified duration below Macaulay for every bond.

Frequently asked questions

What does it not do?

Each bond is discounted at a single flat yield, not off a zero curve, and portfolio yields are estimated from coupon and market price rather than solved. Scenarios use the duration and convexity approximation, total return assumes constant yields and reinvestment rates, and the default case applies one recovery rate to every bond.

Can I load my own portfolio?

Yes. The portfolio table on INPUTS holds 30 bonds: replace the names, ISINs, terms, sectors, ratings and prices with your own holdings.

Which currency is it in?

The demo is a sterling portfolio of gilts and UK credit. Prices and terms are all inputs, so any currency works if you are consistent.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo portfolio.
