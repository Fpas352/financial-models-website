The problem it solves

A lending budget that applies today's rate to the whole book gets NIM wrong in any rate cycle. Existing loans keep the rate they were written at; only new business reprices. The gap between front book and back book is where margin forecasts fail.

This model tracks every origination month as its own vintage with its rate locked, so the NIM path reflects how the book actually reprices, and links it through to provisions and capital.

Who it's for

FP&A and finance teams at banks, building societies and specialist lenders building the lending budget or plan. Treasury and ALM teams forecasting NIM. Lending heads who need volume, margin, losses and capital in one view.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INSTRUCTIONS

How to run the model and common uses: budget, stress, board reporting, ICAAP support.

INPUTS

Opening balances, origination volumes, contract rates and rate trajectory, prepayment and default rates, LGD, stage allocations, risk weights, funding costs, opex and tax, with a Base, Upside and Downside selector.

ENGINE

Monthly roll-forward for eight products: residential, buy-to-let, CRE, SME, corporate, consumer unsecured, cards and government-guaranteed lending.

VINTAGES

Cohort matrix: each origination month locks its rate and amortises on its own.

INCOME

Monthly P&L from interest through cost of funds, fees, opex and provisions to net profit.

ECL

IFRS 9 Stage 1, 2 and 3 provisions, coverage ratios and cost of risk.

RWA

Standardised credit RWA by product and operational RWA.

ANALYTICS

NIM decomposition, portfolio mix, asset quality and yield waterfall.

SENSITIVITY

Two-way tables for NIM and net profit against rates, default and prepayment.

OUTPUTS

Executive dashboard with charts.

CHECKS

16 integrity checks, including vintage interest tying to the engine and stage allocations summing to 100%.

What makes it different

Rate-locked vintages

Interest is calculated on each vintage at the rate it was originated, so a rising or falling rate trajectory flows through to new business while the back book holds its rate.

A full roll-forward per product

Opening, originations, scheduled repayment, prepayment, default, recovery, closing. Prepayment and default use annual CPR and CDR converted to monthly rates.

From volume to capital

Balances drive the P&L, the IFRS 9 provision and standardised RWA, so a change in origination plans shows up in margin, losses and capital together.

NIM you can explain

ANALYTICS breaks NIM down into yield, funding cost and mix, so you can tell the board why it moved.

Eight UK-style products

Residential and buy-to-let mortgages, CRE, SME, corporate, unsecured, cards and government-guaranteed schemes, each with its own assumptions.

Frequently asked questions

How detailed is the IFRS 9 calculation?

Stage allocations are percentages by product, with ECL as balance times PD times LGD and a lifetime approximation for Stage 2. For loan-by-loan staging, see the IFRS 9 ECL Model: Loan-Level Edition.

Can I change the products?

Every volume, rate and risk assumption is set per product on INPUTS, and the eight rows can be relabelled for the lending products you run.

Does it handle changing interest rates?

Yes. A rate trajectory sets the rate on new originations year by year, and each vintage keeps its own rate.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo bank, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
