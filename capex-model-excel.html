Capital Expenditure Planning Model

Plan, approve and track a portfolio of 20 capital projects over 60 months: monthly planned and actual spend, budget tracking with cost performance, straight-line depreciation, NPV and payback by project, a funding gap analysis, an approval tracker and a post-completion review.

The problem it solves

A capital programme is approved project by project and then reported as a portfolio, and the two views rarely agree. By the second year nobody can say which projects are over budget, when depreciation will hit the P&L, or whether the programme is still funded.

This model holds both views in one place. Each project's budget, start month, duration, useful life and benefit rate drive its spend, its depreciation and its return, and the portfolio, funding and dashboard tabs add them up.

Who it's for

Finance and capital planning teams. PMO and change teams reporting on a capital programme. CFOs and investment committees approving projects. FP&A analysts forecasting depreciation.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Scenario selector, start date, tax rate, WACC, inflation, contingency and funding assumptions, and for each of 20 projects: category, budget, start month, duration, useful life, annual benefit rate and hurdle rate, each with Base, Upside, Downside and Stress values.

PROJECT_REGISTER

All 20 projects with sponsor, category, status, budget, actual to date, completion and RAG.

SPEND_PROFILE

Monthly planned and actual spend by project, cumulative and percent complete, and portfolio totals.

BUDGET_TRACKING

Original and revised budget, committed, actual, remaining, estimate at completion and CPI by project and month.

DEPRECIATION

Capitalised amount, monthly straight-line depreciation, accumulated depreciation and net book value by project, starting after completion.

PROJECT_RETURNS

Investment, annual benefit and operating cost, NPV at the WACC, an IRR proxy, payback and profitability index, with five-year cash flows by project.

PORTFOLIO

Capex by category, year and status, and the growth against maintenance split.

FUNDING

Internal cash, debt, equity and disposals against the capex programme and interest, with the annual and cumulative funding position.

APPROVAL

Approval tracker: each project's IRR proxy against the WACC and payback against five years, giving approve, review or reject.

POST_COMPLETION

Budget against actual cost and expected against actual benefit for each project, with expected and actual ROI.

DASHBOARD

KPIs and chart data for spend by category and year, project returns against hurdle, and cumulative spend.

SENSITIVITY

Portfolio NPV by cost overrun and benefit shortfall, and grids on hurdle rate and budget.

CHECKS

72 integrity checks, including portfolio totals equal to the sum of projects and each project's variance equal to budget less actual.

What makes it different

Project to portfolio

Twenty projects, each with its own budget, timing, useful life and benefit rate, roll up to portfolio views by category, year and status.

Depreciation that follows delivery

Each project is capitalised when it completes and depreciated straight-line over its useful life, so the depreciation profile follows the delivery plan.

Budget tracking with CPI

Original and revised budget, commitments, actuals, estimate at completion and a cost performance index for every project.

A funding check

Sources of funding against the capex programme and interest each year, with the cumulative surplus or gap.

From approval to review

An approval rule on the IRR proxy and payback, and a post-completion review of cost and benefit against the business case.

Frequently asked questions

What does it not do?

Planned spend is spread evenly over each project's duration. Depreciation is straight-line with no residual value. The IRR column is a proxy (annual net benefit over investment), payback is simple, internal cash generation is constant each year, and interest is charged on the full debt facility, which is conservative.

Where do I enter actual spend?

On SPEND_PROFILE, one actual row per project per month. Budget tracking, CPI, the register and the dashboard update from there.

What do the scenarios change?

Every project assumption and the global rates carry Base, Upside, Downside and Stress values, switched from one selector. The SENSITIVITY tables run independently of the selector.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Yes. The free sample shows every tab, chart and check with real results on the demo bank, plus one engine tab with live formulas.
