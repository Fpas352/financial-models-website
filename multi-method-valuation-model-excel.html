The problem it solves

Every valuation method answers a slightly different question, and a number from one method alone invites the obvious challenge: what do the others say? Building five or six separate models to answer that, and keeping them consistent, takes days.

This model runs all six from one set of inputs, so the ranges are consistent, the weights are explicit and the football field is ready for the committee.

Who it's for

Corporate finance and transaction advisory teams. Private equity and corporate development. Finance teams preparing fairness, impairment or shareholder valuations. Candidates preparing for valuation case studies.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Target financials and WACC assumptions, with Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

DCF

Ten-year unlevered free cash flow, with Gordon growth and exit multiple terminal values blended.

TRADING_COMPS

Fifteen comparable companies on EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT, P/E and P/B.

PRECEDENT_TXNS

Fifteen transactions with control premium analysis.

DDM

Two-stage dividend discount with a ten-year explicit forecast.

LBO

Five-year buyout with three debt tranches, a cash sweep and IRR sensitivity to entry and exit multiples.

ASSET_BASED

Book value, adjusted net assets, liquidation value and replacement cost.

WEIGHTED_VALUATION

Method weights and the blended value.

FOOTBALL_FIELD

Valuation ranges across all six methods.

EQUITY_BRIDGE

Enterprise value to equity value.

DASHBOARD

Charts and summary metrics.

SENSITIVITY

Two-way tables, plus extended sensitivities.

CHECKS

115 integrity checks.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from buying the separate models?

The separate DCF, Trading Comps, Precedent Transactions, DDM and LBO models each go deeper on their method. This model runs simplified versions of all six together from one set of inputs, for a consistent range.

What are the limits?

Point-in-time data, a simplified LBO with a cash sweep, and a DDM that suits regular dividend payers. Each method's tab states its assumptions.

Which currency is it in?

The demo is in US dollars, millions. All values are inputs.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo company.
