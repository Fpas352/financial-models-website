Buy-to-Let Property Investment Model

Ten rental properties in one workbook: acquisition costs and stamp duty, a monthly mortgage schedule for each, annual P&L and cash flow, value and equity growth, yields and equity multiples, and a side-by-side of owning personally or through a limited company.

The problem it solves

Since Section 24, the same rent can leave a higher-rate landlord with much less than a company owner keeps, and a portfolio spread across cities is hard to judge property by property on the back of an envelope.

This model runs each property from purchase price and rent to net cash flow and equity, then compares personal ownership, with mortgage interest relieved by a 20% tax credit, against a company paying corporation tax and dividend tax on extraction.

Who it's for

Landlords and property investors, with one property or a portfolio. Mortgage brokers and property advisers modelling options for clients. Accountants comparing personal and company ownership.

What's in the model

Every workbook opens on a COVER tab with instructions and a linked tab index. Then:

INPUTS

Income tax, CGT, corporation tax, allowances and dividend rates; acquisition fees and the additional property surcharge; and for each of ten properties: price, deposit, mortgage rate and term, rent, voids, agent fee, service charge, ground rent, insurance, maintenance, house price growth and rental growth. Base, Upside, Downside and Stress scenarios.

ACQUISITION_COSTS

Deposit, mortgage, stamp duty, legal, survey, broker and valuation fees, total equity required and LTV per property.

SDLT_CALCULATOR

Stamp duty band by band for each property, plus the additional property surcharge and the effective rate.

MORTGAGE_SCHEDULE

Monthly repayment schedule for all ten mortgages: payment, interest, principal and balance.

ANNUAL_PL

Rent less voids and agent fees, mortgage interest and running costs, with the Section 24 credit and tax, per property.

CASH_FLOW

Annual cash in and out per property, cumulative cash flow and cash yield on equity.

VALUATION

Value with house price growth, mortgage outstanding, equity, LTV, gross yield and cumulative capital gain.

RETURNS

Gross and net yield, cash-on-cash, total return and equity multiple at 5, 10 and 25 years, per property.

TAX_OPTIMISATION

Personal ownership against a limited company for the portfolio: Section 24, personal allowance and bands, corporation tax and dividend tax on extraction.

PORTFOLIO_SUMMARY

All ten properties in one view, with weighted LTV, regions, flats against houses, and total value, equity and rent.

DASHBOARD

Chart data for equity build-up, cash flow, value against debt, yields, mortgage balances and the tax comparison.

SENSITIVITY

Five-year return by house price growth and yield, mortgage payment by rate and LTV, and net cash flow by void rate and mortgage rate.

CHECKS

62 integrity checks, including each mortgage repaid to zero at term and total principal repaid equal to the original loan.

What makes it different

Section 24 built in

For personal ownership, mortgage interest is not deducted from rental profit; relief comes as a 20% tax credit, property by property and for the portfolio.

Personal or company, side by side

The same portfolio taxed both ways: income tax with Section 24 against corporation tax and dividend tax on extracting the profit, with the annual difference.

Stamp duty by band

Each band calculated separately, with the additional property surcharge as an input.

A mortgage schedule for every property

Monthly repayment schedules on the annuity method, with interest on the prior month's balance.

Returns over three horizons

Yield, cash-on-cash, total return and equity multiple at 5, 10 and 25 years, so near-term cash flow and long-term equity are both visible.

Frequently asked questions

Which mortgage type does it model?

Repayment mortgages on the annuity method. Refinancing and remortgaging are not modelled in this version.

What does it not do?

Stamp duty uses the SDLT bands on SDLT_CALCULATOR for every property, so Scottish LBTT and Welsh LTT are not calculated. Service charges and insurance rise at a fixed 3% a year, ground rent is held flat, and the company route assumes all profit is extracted as dividends.

Are there macros?

No. Open formulas, no VBA. Every number traces back to an input.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. The free sample is the complete workbook with every formula and check live, inputs locked, so you evaluate it on the demo portfolio.
